The strategy in brief

  • Scope: Kalshi KXMLBGAME moneyline only. No NRFI, first-five, props, totals, or run lines.
  • Model: Elo baseline with a GameScore blend — team Elo 35%, starter 30%, bullpen 15%, lineup 10%, park 5%, rest and travel 5%. Logistic conversion, isotonic calibration (Platt fallback), minimum 1,000 games of history.
  • Edge: calibrated model probability minus the executable Kalshi ask. Required edge is the tier minimum or the fee estimate plus a 2-point uncertainty buffer, whichever is larger. Tiers by model probability: C (52 to under 60) needs 7 points, A (60 to under 75) needs 5, B (75 to 88) needs 4. A price below 50 cents adds 2.5 points. Edge above 18 points is rejected as suspicious — except a price of 45 cents or less, which instead requires a 20-point edge.
  • Fees: estimated at 0.07 × price × (1 − price) per contract. After fees, a gross edge needs about 5.6% at 20 cents, 3.5% at 50 cents, and 1.4% at 80 cents just to break even.
  • Sizing: quarter Kelly, tier position caps of 5% / 4% / 3% of bankroll, square-root haircut for concurrent positions, no averaging down, manual kill switch.
  • Guards: model probability must sit between 52% and 88%; spread at most 4 cents or 6%; starter, bullpen, lineup, injuries, park, current price, resolution rules, and order book all confirmed first.

Known open items: the config's 5/4/3 percent tier caps conflict with desk knob sheets recording 9.13/7.30/5.48 percent — which authority the live bot runs is unresolved, and the config hash above is how we will know. The trade journal's MLB entries are not yet reconciled against settlements, so the settled record is unknown — not zero.
